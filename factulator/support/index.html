Factulator support

How can we help?

Find answers about calculations, facts, history, saved items, sources, and advertising privacy, or send a support request.

A generated fact appeared

Factulator always keeps mathematical facts available offline. When a sourced fact exists, the app downloads it and replaces the generated fact. A slow or unavailable connection may delay that update.

See another fact

Use the refresh control to rotate through available facts. Dense values such as calendar years can have many cultural, historical, sports, and biographical facts.

Decimal results

Factulator calculates fractional results and requests sourced facts for the nearest integer. Generated explanations can also describe the decimal as a fraction or percentage.

History or saved facts

Calculation history and saved facts are stored locally. If they disappear after deleting the app, they cannot be recovered from a Farr Out Apps account because no account or synchronization service is provided.

Advertising privacy

Factulator requests contextual, non-personalized ads and does not ask to track you across apps. Regional choices are available under Factulator Settings when required.

Still need help?

Open a Factulator support request →

Public GitHub issues can be read by anyone. Do not include calculations, ad identifiers, or other sensitive information.

Before contacting support

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Factulator version, and a concise description of what happened. If a fact appears incorrect, include the number, fact text, and source URL, but remove personal information from screenshots.

Privacy questions

Read the Factulator Privacy Policy. Privacy questions can use the same support form, but do not post sensitive information in a public issue.
