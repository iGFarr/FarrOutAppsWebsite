Help center

How can we help?

Find quick answers for Universal Remote or send a support request through the project’s issue tracker.

TV not appearing?

Confirm your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, allow Local Network access in iOS Settings, and keep the TV awake for its first connection.

Pairing help

Select the TV, tap Connect, and follow the code or approval prompt shown on the television. Pairing credentials are saved securely on your iPhone.

LG power-on

Enable “Mobile TV On” or “Turn on via Wi-Fi” on the LG TV. In the remote, open LG Power-On Setup and enter the Wi-Fi MAC address shown in the TV’s network settings if needed.

Still need help?

Open a support request on GitHub →

Never include pairing codes, passwords, tokens, or other secrets.

Before contacting support

Please include the TV manufacturer and model, its operating system version if known, your iPhone model and iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are welcome after removing private network addresses, serial numbers, and other personal information.

Privacy questions

Read the Universal Remote Privacy Policy. Privacy-related questions can be submitted through the same support request form; do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
