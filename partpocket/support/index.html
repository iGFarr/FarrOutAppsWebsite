PartPocket support

How can we help?

Find answers for scanning labels, iCloud synchronization, due dates, and notifications, or send a support request.

Scanning a label

Allow Camera access, point at a barcode or part number, and tap the recognized result. Always confirm scanned text before saving. You can enter every field manually if scanning is unavailable.

iCloud not syncing?

Confirm you are signed in to the same Apple Account on each device, iCloud Drive is enabled, and PartPocket is allowed to use iCloud. Keep the app open briefly on a reliable connection after making changes.

Reminder not appearing?

Open the item and verify it has a last-replaced date, replacement interval, and enabled reminder. Then check PartPocket under iOS Settings → Notifications.

Removing saved data

Delete an individual item from its detail screen. Delete a home from the Homes list to remove that home’s areas, items, photos, history, and scheduled reminders.

Advertising privacy

PartPocket requests contextual, non-personalized ads and does not ask to track you across apps. Regional privacy choices appear under PartPocket Settings when required.

Still need help?

Open a PartPocket support request →

Public GitHub issues can be read by anyone. Never attach household photos, addresses, Apple Account details, or other sensitive information.

Before contacting support

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, PartPocket version, and a short description of what happened. For synchronization problems, say whether both devices use the same Apple Account and whether iCloud Drive is enabled. Remove personal details from screenshots.

Privacy questions

Read the PartPocket Privacy Policy. Privacy-related questions can use the same support form, but do not post sensitive information in a public issue.
